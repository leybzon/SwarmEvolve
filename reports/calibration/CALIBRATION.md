## 2. Noise and power

- **pairing**: d across seeds for a non-mirror baseline pairing, centred and symmetrised: the seed-to-seed spread between two programs of different strength.

Power: probability that the acceptance rule (mean d > 0 and one-sided sign-flip p < 0.1) accepts, from 2000 simulated evaluations per cell, 2000 random sign flips each. A true difference is imposed by exponentially tilting the symmetric noise distribution to that mean, which keeps d inside its range. The 0.0 column is the false-acceptance rate when there is no difference. Blank: the model has zero spread or cannot reach that mean.
